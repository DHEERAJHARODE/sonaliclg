Admissions

Admissions

Welcome to the Admission Portal of Sonali College, Guna, a premier institution committed to excellence in teacher education. The college is recognised by NCTE and affiliated to Kranti Veer Tatya Tope University, Guna, ensuring high academic standards.

We offer comprehensive teacher-training programs designed to develop skilled, confident, and socially responsible educators.

Courses Offered

1. B.A. B.Ed. (Integrated 4-Year Program)

A dual-degree program combining Arts education with professional teacher training.
Eligibility: 10+2 (any stream)
Highlights: Integrated curriculum, pedagogy training, practical teaching experience

2. B.Ed. (2-Year Program)

A professional program for graduates aiming to become trained teachers.
Eligibility: Graduation in any discipline
Highlights: Modern teaching methods, classroom management, internships

3. M.Ed. (2-Year Program)

An advanced program for future academic leaders and researchers.
Eligibility: B.Ed. from a recognized institution
Highlights: Research work, dissertation, leadership training
