About Our College

SONALI COLLEGE - GUNA was established In 1978. It is approved by NCTE (National Council for Teacher Education) & affiliated by Jiwaji University, Gwalior. The campus of the SONALI COLLEGE - GUNA is located in Guna, Madhya Pradesh. The Institute can be reached either by rail or by road.
