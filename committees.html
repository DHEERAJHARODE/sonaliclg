Cells & Committees

Cells & Committees

Career Guidance Cell

The college runs a Career Guidance Cell under the supervision of experienced faculty members. It regularly organizes expert lectures to guide students about various career opportunities in today’s competitive job market. Students’ queries and concerns are given top priority.

Placement Cell

The Placement Cell works in coordination with the Career Guidance Cell and keeps track of student placements. It provides updated information about job opportunities and helps students secure placements. Students who get placed are required to submit their details to maintain alumni records.

Vigilance & Grievance Redressal Cell

To maintain a healthy academic environment, the college has a Vigilance and Grievance Redressal Cell. It ensures discipline within the campus and addresses students’ grievances related to academics and other issues effectively.

Research Advisory Committee

The Research Advisory Committee includes members from both teaching and non-teaching staff. It promotes research activities and supports students and faculty in research-related work, creating a strong academic and research culture within the institution.
